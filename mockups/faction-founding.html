Player factions — the design space

Lore-bible §9: a faction is a declared want, founding is a naming-event, and the machinery already treats factions as plain data. The sample faction throughout: the Reef Wardens — want: the reefs back. Sections: §1 the founding flow (common to all directions), §2 the three directions, §3 the Rep tab, §4 world surfaces. ★ = recommended.

§1 — The charter desk (founding flow, common)

Lives in the Shipyard district, beside the mechanic's bench — you walk to it (Slice B's door pattern). Three declarations and a fee. The warning is real: the chronicle is permanent.

"Names are cheap. Names in the ledger aren't. Say what your crew wants and sign."
⚓ a place — hold a site the reach forgot
⇄ a trade — move what nobody else will
☠ a grudge — break the cartel that broke you
✎ our own words… (freeform — fork 4)
15,000 cr — the desk keeps it either way
⚠ founding is chronicled: broadcast to every pilot, written in the galaxy's ledger, never erased. Disbanding doesn't unwrite it.

§2 — Three directions (what a faction DOES, v1)

A — The Banner

identity-first
  • Name, color, want, roster
  • Your color on your ghost's name tag
  • Deeds credit the banner in the chronicle ("…for the Reef Wardens")
  • Faction card in the Rep tab
Cheapest. Risk: a clan tag — the want is declared but never does anything.

★ B — The Claim

territory-first
  • Everything in A, plus one claim per faction: plant your mark at a charted, unoccupied site
  • The site rings in your color on every map
  • The cartels' daily occupation roll prefers claimed sites — your flag is an invitation to a fight
  • Repelling them chronicles as the faction's deed
  • Salvage stays first-come for everyone — the claim is a flag and a fight-magnet, not a paywall
Reuses poiState / occupations / liberation wholesale. Turns the living-world loop two-way: the world finally pushes back on something players chose.

C — The Tally

want-first
  • Everything in A, plus the want is counted: haul-a-good / break-raids / chart-and-salvage
  • Member deeds tick the faction's tally
  • Thresholds chronicle milestones ("the Wardens have moved 500 crates of panacea")
Most faithful to rule 4, but hooks span trade + combat + salvage paths, and a counter is less visible than a flag on the map. Better as slice 2, once claims give the want somewhere to live.
