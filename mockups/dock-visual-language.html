The goods glyph language + dock districts

Mockup for the visuals-over-text milestone. Pinned forks: Ship tab dissolves into the interactive schematic (log merges into the Log tab) · Dock + Shipyard districts (crew hires dockside) · goods glyphs first, planet/rank insignia in later slices · deal bars compare against YOUR ledger (scouting builds your market sense). Rule that makes the language learnable: glyph + color always travel together; the text name lives one layer down — hover anything to see its under-the-hood card.

4 · The Ship tab dissolves — the schematic gets an under-the-hood layer

Mods pin onto the slot they modify (◈ on the weapon mount, ◈ on the cargo bay); the name engraves on the hull. Click a pin — the under-the-hood card is the gearhead layer, and it's the ONLY place numbers live. The old Ship tab retires; the ship log's journal lines move to the Log tab (your journal above, the galaxy's chronicle below — one history surface).
